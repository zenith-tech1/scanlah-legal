Why is the text on my scan wrong or missing?

Good light and a flat, unfolded page make the biggest difference. Retake the page, or use Rotate if it came out sideways. Text recognition can make mistakes, so check important numbers and names.

Does ScanLah need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Your documents are stored only on your phone, so keep your own backups (use Back up in the app).

How do I cancel ScanLah Pro?

In iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ScanLah. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple.
