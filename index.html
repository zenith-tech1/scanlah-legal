Privacy Policy

ScanLah

PDF document scanner

EFFECTIVE 07 NOV 2026 VER 1.0
In short
  • ScanLah has no account, no login and no server of ours. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Your scanned pictures, the text read from them and your PDFs stay on your phone. We never see them, and they are never uploaded for reading.
  • Your documents leave your phone only when you choose to: Share, Save to Files or Back up.
  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some data to do that. Pro and the free trial show no ads. Details in Section 1.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. ScanLah checks on your phone whether you have Pro; we receive no payment details or purchase history.
01

Information we collect

Stored only on your phone

ScanLah keeps the following in its own storage on your phone, never on a server we operate:

  • The pictures of the pages you scan, and the text read from them
  • Document names, tags and folders, and the PDFs you export
  • App settings and preferences

Deleting a document in ScanLah removes it from your phone, and deleting the app removes everything it stored. If your phone backs up to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include this data as part of your phone's normal backup, which is managed by Apple's iOS settings, not by ScanLah.

Camera and photos

When you allow camera access, ScanLah uses it to scan pages. Text is read from your pages on your phone itself (using Apple's text recognition, or Google's ML Kit text recognition where Apple's is not available); the images are never uploaded for this. If you choose "Choose from Photos", only the pictures you pick are used, and the same on-device processing applies.

Advertising data (Google AdMob, free version)

The free version of ScanLah shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect:

  • your phone's advertising identifier and similar device identifiers
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address
  • information about the ads you were shown and how you interact with the app and the ads (for example taps and video views)
  • performance data such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use, and crash logs

This collection is governed by Google's own policies, not ours. See how Google uses data in advertising. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, ScanLah shows a consent message so you can choose whether to allow this. On iPhone, ScanLah also asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites; if you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. Pro and the free trial show no ads.

Subscriptions (ScanLah Pro)

Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. ScanLah checks on your phone, through Apple, whether you have Pro; that information is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Nothing else

ScanLah has no analytics service of its own, no crash-reporting service of ours and no sign-in. We do not sell data.

02

How we use this information

To let you scan, read, search, store and export your documents on your phone; to show ads that keep the free version free; and to check your Pro status. We do not use your information for anything beyond what is described in this policy.

03

Sharing

We do not share your documents with anyone, because we have no access to them. They leave your phone only when you choose Share, Save to Files or Back up, and then they go where you send them (for example Google Drive or iCloud), under that service's own rules. Advertising data is shared with Google and its advertising partners as described in Section 1, and purchases are handled by Apple under its own rules.

04

Your choices

  • Delete your data — delete documents inside ScanLah, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
  • Ads and tracking — answer the consent message (EEA, UK, Switzerland) and the tracking prompt, and change tracking anytime under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Subscribing to Pro, or being in the free trial, removes ads.
  • Camera and Photos access — change these anytime under iOS Settings → ScanLah.
05

Children's privacy

ScanLah is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

06

International users

ScanLah is made in Singapore and available worldwide. Google and Apple may process data in other countries under their own policies. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the choices in this policy, including the consent message, apply to you.

07

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ScanLah changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
